What this means

On a $30,000 campaign with 200 backers: fees = $1,500 (platform) + $900 (processing) + $40 (per-txn) = $2,440 in fees. Direct-to-consumer via Shopify costs ~1.7–2.9% (Stripe) with no platform cut.
